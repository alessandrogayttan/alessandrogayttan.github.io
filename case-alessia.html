Client goals

Inpulso 43 needed to scale customer reception without hiring more staff. Patients message at all hours — booking evaluations, asking prices, joining workshop waitlists. The team couldn't respond to everyone manually.

Problem

  • Dozens of WhatsApp messages daily with repetitive questions
  • Manual appointment scheduling in Google Calendar
  • No automated reminders or waitlist management
  • Website buttons opened generic WhatsApp — no context, no tracking

Solution

Alessia is a conversational AI built with Google Gemini and Flask. It runs on DigitalOcean with Docker and connects to WhatsApp Business API, Google Calendar, Google Sheets, and a SQLite database.

WhatsApp reception

Handles patient messages 24/7 with warm, human-like responses in Spanish.

Business integrations

The assistant connects to Google Calendar, Sheets, and SQLite-backed workflows through its tools.

25+ business tools

Book, reschedule, cancel appointments; manage workshops; confirm payments; escalate to humans.

Automated jobs

Appointment reminders, waitlist notifications, and scheduled background tasks.

Modular Python monolith: webhook handler, AI conversation layer, business tools, and job scheduler — deployed with Gunicorn + Docker on DigitalOcean.

Engineering decisions & tradeoffs

  • Python + Flask — Fast to iterate, excellent for AI integrations and webhooks
  • Gemini — Strong Spanish conversation and reliable function calling for business tools
  • SQLite — Lightweight persistence for patient memory and job queues at this scale
  • Docker — Consistent runtime packaging on DigitalOcean. The current deployment uses one web worker while the scheduler remains inside the app.
  • Scheduler boundary — The repository documents moving scheduled jobs into a separate worker before adding web workers, to avoid duplicate reminders.
